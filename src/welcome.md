# Welcome

This is a plain text editor. There are no fonts to pick, no margins to drag and no toolbar to configure. You write, and the editor gets out of your way. #welcome

## Focus

Press **⌘D** to switch on Focus Mode. Everything except the sentence you are writing fades into the background. Change the scope to a whole paragraph in the *View* menu (the three dots in the top right).

Typewriter Mode (**⌥⌘T**) keeps the line you are typing in the middle of the window.

## Syntax and Style Check

Turn on parts of speech in the *View* menu. The beautiful old house quietly stood on the hill, and its windows glowed warmly.

Style Check marks fillers, clichés and redundancies. It is actually very useful, and at the end of the day it will basically make your writing really much better. Our future plans include an added bonus.

## Authors

Text pasted with **⇧⌘V** is marked as written by AI and shown in color. Characters you type into it become yours. Authorship is saved at the end of a downloaded file, so it travels with the document.

## Markdown

- Lists continue when you press Return
- [x] Tasks can be checked with **⌘L**
- [ ] ==Highlights==, ~~strikethrough~~ and `inline code`
- Links to other notes: [[Shortcuts]]

> [!TIP]
> Callouts start with `[!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!WARNING]` or `[!CAUTION]`.

| Feature | Keys |
| ------- | :--: |
| Preview | ⌘R   |
| Library | ⇧⌘L  |

Press Tab inside a table to move between cells. The *Format* menu (the **Aa** button) adds, moves, sorts and aligns rows and columns.

Math is written in TeX: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, or as a block:

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

Footnotes keep asides out of the way.[^1]
